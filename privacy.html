Privacy Policy

Video Publisher · Effective November 7, 2026

Video Publisher is a private, self-hosted tool that its operator uses to publish and manage videos on the operator's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public, and only the channel owner signs in to it. This policy explains what the app accesses through Google and how that information is handled.

Information the app accesses

When the channel owner connects a YouTube channel through Google sign-in, the app requests these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload videos the owner produced.
  • Manage the YouTube account (youtube.force-ssl): to set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists and publishing schedules on the owner's channels.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to confirm the selected channel and check the status of uploaded videos.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read view, watch-time and audience statistics for the owner's own videos.

The app does not access the owner's email, contacts, files or any Google data beyond these YouTube permissions, and it never accesses other people's accounts.

How the information is used

  • Solely to publish and manage videos on the owner's own channels and to understand how those videos perform, so future videos can be planned and improved.
  • Channel and video statistics may be included in requests to AI service providers configured by the owner, only to plan and produce the owner's own content.
  • The information is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone for their own purposes.

Storage and security

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in an encrypted store whose key is protected by the operating system.
  • Statistics and video records are kept in a local database on the same computer. Nothing is stored on a public server.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the owner uses the app. Disconnecting a channel in the app deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; revoking access stops the app from reaching the channel immediately.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be raised through the issues page of this site's GitHub repository.
